Paris is easyFontainebleau-Avon to Paris Gare de Lyon: about 40 min on the Transilien R train.
PricesFull adult rates, which is what US visitors pay. Several museums are free on Sunday 6 December.
Museum closing daysLouvre, Orangerie and Château de Fontainebleau close Tuesdays. Orsay and Versailles close Mondays.
Crowds rise Dec 19Paris school break starts Saturday 19 December. Book big sights before then if possible.
